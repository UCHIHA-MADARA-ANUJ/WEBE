## How to use this

- **⬥  You can skip anything.**
  Every question is optional. Skipping one changes what the page says, never whether it works.

- **⬥  “None yet” is a real answer.**
  You are in first year. A page that admits that and then shows what you are reaching for is stronger than one padded with fake projects.

- **⬥  The starred questions matter most.**
  They set the structure. Everything else is decoration on top.

- **⬥  Answer in your own words.**
  I would rather use a slightly awkward sentence you actually wrote than a smooth one that sounds like every other profile.

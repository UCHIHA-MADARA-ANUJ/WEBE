One to three. For each: a name, the year, what it does in one sentence, and what you built it with.

## 2. Internships

Any, including short or unpaid ones. Role, company, dates, and two bullet points on what you actually did.

## 3. Certifications

Any completed course with a certificate, plus the issuer and the year. The cybersecurity course goes here once finished.

## 4. CGPA or percentage

## 5. Links

GitHub, LinkedIn, or a portfolio, if she has them.

## 6. Objective

The current paragraph is factual. If she is actively looking for an internship, say so and it changes to a targeted line.
